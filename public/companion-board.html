Burnbot on your desktop

Three ways for the robot's face to turn into a screen when you point at it. Every robot on this page is the real desktop robot at the size it stands on your screen (Medium unless marked). All three ship: pick yours in the robot's right-click menu or Robot → Hover Screen. Big shows the most, Wide takes the least height, Compact the least room.

About 50 seconds of a working session, played on a busy desktop. Pick a hover screen, or play all three in turn. Move your own pointer onto the stage and you can hover and click the robot yourself; the scripted pointer steps aside.
