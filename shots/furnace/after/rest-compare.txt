At-rest comparison: HEAD's public/ (served from a copy) against the working tree, in headless
Chromium. The clock is fake and paused, Math.random is seeded, and a warm-up page loads first.
Hashes are of the robot canvas.

68 jobs, 218 frames:
- 7 states at scales 2, 3 and 4
- right=0 (robot on the other side)
- Reduce Motion
- &progress=0.6 at rest, which must not show the bar
- ?demo=moments n=0..11, which covers the expressions, bubbles and the panel

Result: every frame is identical, except for the five `asleep` jobs. Their hash varies from run to run
even on HEAD alone: the calm 80 ms frame timer lands in a different phase, which moves the zzz. Each
tree was run 4 times on those jobs. Every hash the working tree produced also occurs in HEAD's own set,
so asleep is identical too.
